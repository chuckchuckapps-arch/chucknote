Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

ChuckNote is built with privacy as a default. Your notes stay on your device. We don't run servers, we don't have accounts, and we don't sell your data.

What we collect

ChuckNote does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. All notes, attachments, and location pins are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party data sharing of any kind.

Location

If you choose to pin a note to a place, ChuckNote requests your precise location to attach coordinates to that note. This data stays entirely on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. Location access is requested only when you explicitly initiate a pin.

Onboarding survey

ChuckNote asks a few optional questions during onboarding (use case, experience level, goal). Your answers are stored locally on your device in UserDefaults and are never transmitted anywhere.

iCloud backup

Your notes may be included in your iCloud backup if you have iCloud Backup enabled on your device. This is controlled entirely by your iOS settings and Apple's iCloud service.

Children

ChuckNote is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
