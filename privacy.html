Chuck Note

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026  ·  Version 1.0

Short version: Chuck Note is a local-first notes app. Your notes live on your device. We do not have a server, we do not collect your notes, and we do not share any personal data with third parties. The only data that ever leaves your device is what Apple processes for subscriptions on our behalf.

1. Who We Are

Chuck Note ("we", "us", "our") is an iOS application developed and published by Chuck Silver. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at chuckchuckapps@gmail.com.

2. Data We Collect — and Data We Don't

Chuck Note is designed to collect as little as possible. The table below describes every category of data the app touches.

Data How it's used Stored where Shared?
Your notes & attachments Core app functionality On your device (SwiftData) Never
App preferences (dark mode, etc.) Remember your settings On your device (UserDefaults) Never
Note location (optional, coarse) Pin notes to a map place On your device Never
Exported files (PDF / TXT) Share sheet only Temp folder, auto-deleted by iOS Only if you share them
Purchase & subscription info Unlock Premium features Apple's servers Apple only (see §5)

We do not collect analytics, crash logs, advertising identifiers, device fingerprints, or any data for tracking or profiling. We have no back-end server and receive no telemetry from the app.

3. Location Access

Chuck Note requests When In Use location access only when you choose to pin a note to your current location. The location is stored as a latitude/longitude pair and a plain-text place name (e.g., "San Francisco") on your device. It is never transmitted to us. You may revoke location access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Chuck Note.

4. Photos and Files

When you attach a photo or file to a note, it is read directly from your device and stored locally inside the app's SwiftData container. No copy is uploaded anywhere. Exported PDFs and text files are written to iOS's temporary directory and deleted by the operating system; they are only shared if you explicitly use the share sheet.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

Chuck Note Premium is sold through Apple's App Store using StoreKit 2. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your credit card number, Apple ID, or any billing details. The only subscription data the app processes is a cryptographically signed transaction receipt from Apple, used solely to verify that Premium is active on your account.

Apple's privacy practices are governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

Chuck Note is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in the EU/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that raises a privacy concern, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

All your data lives on your device. To delete everything, delete the Chuck Note app. iOS will remove all associated SwiftData files, UserDefaults entries, and cached temporary files.

Because we hold no server-side copy of your data, we cannot recover notes after the app is deleted unless you have an iCloud or device backup.

8. iCloud Sync (Future Feature)

A future version of Chuck Note may offer optional iCloud sync for Premium subscribers. If and when that feature ships, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe exactly what is synced and how it is protected. Until then, no data leaves your device through iCloud.

9. Third-Party Services

Chuck Note does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, social, or tracking SDKs. The only third-party system the app communicates with is Apple's StoreKit and App Store infrastructure for subscription management.

10. Security

Your notes are stored in the app's SwiftData container, which is protected by iOS's standard file-system encryption (Data Protection). Files are encrypted at rest whenever the device is locked.

The "Protected" flag on individual notes adds a deletion confirmation prompt. It does not apply additional encryption or require authentication to view the note's content.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app adds new features (such as iCloud sync or widgets). When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. For material changes, we will provide an in-app notice.
