Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

ChuckNote is built with privacy as a default. Your notes stay on your device. We don't run servers, we don't have accounts, and we don't sell your data.

What we collect

ChuckNote itself does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. All notes, attachments, and location pins are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

Location

If you choose to pin a note to a place, ChuckNote requests your location to attach coordinates to that note. This data stays entirely on your device. We never transmit your location to any server.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

ChuckNote is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may use device identifiers (including Apple's Advertising Identifier, IDFA) to show you relevant ads.

When you first open ChuckNote, you'll be shown Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. If you allow tracking, Google may use your IDFA to personalize ads. If you decline, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized.

Google's use of your data is governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data in apps

To opt out of personalized ads on your device, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and disable tracking for ChuckNote, or go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising and turn off Personalized Ads.

Onboarding survey

ChuckNote asks a few optional questions during onboarding (use case, experience level, goal). Your answers are stored locally on your device in UserDefaults and are never transmitted anywhere.

Third-party SDKs

ChuckNote uses the following third-party SDK:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) — for displaying banner ads. Google's SDK includes its own privacy manifest disclosing its data practices to Apple.

iCloud backup

Your notes may be included in your iCloud backup if you have iCloud Backup enabled on your device. This is controlled entirely by your iOS settings and Apple's iCloud service.

Children

ChuckNote is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
